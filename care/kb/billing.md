# Billing and payments

## Late payment fee
A $7 late payment fee is added when a bill is not paid by its due date. The first late fee is waived on request if the customer has had no other late fee in the previous billing cycle. Only one waiver is allowed per customer.

## Why a bill changed
A bill can be higher than the previous month because of one-time charges such as roaming passes, a late fee, a new device installment, or an add-on such as premium streaming. Each charge appears as its own line item.

## Autopay discount
Customers on autopay with a debit card or bank account get $5 off per line each month. Credit cards do not qualify for the autopay discount.

## Payment arrangements
A customer who cannot pay in full may split a past-due balance into two payments within 30 days. Payment arrangements are set up by a billing specialist.
