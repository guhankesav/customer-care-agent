# International roaming

## Canada and Mexico
Essentials includes 5 GB of high-speed data per month in Canada and Mexico. Magenta and Magenta MAX include 15 GB. After that, data continues at 256 kbps at no extra charge. Talk and text in Canada and Mexico are unlimited on every plan.

## Roaming day passes
A day pass costs $10 per day and gives unlimited high-speed data for 24 hours in over 210 countries. Day passes are billed as "International roaming" on the next invoice. Passes are added from the account page or by texting PASS to 4357.

## Other countries
Outside Canada and Mexico, Magenta MAX includes 5 GB of high-speed data per month. Other plans include unlimited data at 256 kbps. Calls cost $0.25 per minute.
