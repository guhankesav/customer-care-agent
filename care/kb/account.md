# Account changes

## Cancelling service
Cancellation requests are always transferred to the retention team. Create a retention ticket; do not cancel the account directly. Any remaining device balance becomes due when service is cancelled.

## Identity verification
Before sharing or changing anything on an account, verify the customer with the phone number on the account and the billing ZIP code.

## Plan changes
Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Moving from Magenta to Magenta MAX costs $15 more per line per month.
